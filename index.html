About Island Outpost

A settlement strategy game. Build on your island, match buildings to terrain, feed your villagers and complete goals. Gold and resources are in-game points only. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged.

Ads

An ad is shown only after you press “Watch an ad” in the Market tab. The supplies arrive after the ad finishes. The game is fully playable without ads.

Privacy

Progress is saved on your device. Your Telegram user ID is used only to keep your save separate. We do not sell personal data. Ads are provided by AdsGram, which may process data under its own privacy policy.
